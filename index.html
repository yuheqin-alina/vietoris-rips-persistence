Vietoris–Rips Filtration & Persistent Homology

Interactive visualization of a planar point cloud, its Vietoris–Rips filtration (nested complexes from disks of radius $\varepsilon$), and the associated persistent homology barcode (GUDHI, $\mathbb{Z}_2$).

About this demo

A random point cloud in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and its Vietoris–Rips filtration (nested complexes built from disks of radius $\varepsilon$). Increasing $\varepsilon$ adds simplices when pairwise distances permit.

Designed and developed by Yuhe Qin.

Based on an idea and guidance from Prof. Yifei Zhu

for the Applied Topology Group seminar.

Vertices — sample points. Disks $B(p_i, \varepsilon)$: a 1-simplex (edge) joins $p_i$, $p_j$ when $\|p_i - p_j\| \le 2\varepsilon$; a 2-simplex (triangle) appears when all three pairwise edges exist; a 3-simplex (tetrahedron on four vertices) appears when all six pairwise edges exist.

About this demo

A random point cloud in ℝ² and its Vietoris–Rips filtration (nested complexes built from disks of radius ε). Increasing ε adds simplices when pairwise distances permit.

Designed and developed by Yuhe Qin.

Based on an idea and guidance from Prof. Yifei Zhu

for the Applied Topology Group seminar.

Filtration controls

Range 0–200 (depends on point cloud)

5–30 vertices

Vertices — sample points. Disks B(pi, ε): a 1-simplex (edge) joins pi, pj when ‖pi − pj‖ ≤ 2ε; a 2-simplex (triangle) appears when all three pairwise edges exist; a 3-simplex (tetrahedron on four vertices) appears when all six pairwise edges exist. Each resample uses the selected point cloud model.
